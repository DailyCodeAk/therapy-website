Our Holistic Approach

At Wellness Therapy Center, we believe in treating the whole person—mind, body, and spirit. Our holistic therapy programs integrate traditional psychological approaches with physical wellness practices to create comprehensive healing experiences.

Our physical and holistic therapies recognize that mental and physical health are deeply interconnected. By addressing both dimensions simultaneously, we help clients achieve more complete and lasting wellness.

Benefits of Holistic Therapy

  • ✓ Reduced stress and anxiety
  • ✓ Improved physical well-being
  • ✓ Enhanced mind-body connection
  • ✓ Better sleep quality
  • ✓ Increased emotional resilience
  • ✓ Greater sense of balance and wholeness

Remote Physical Therapy FAQ

Is online physical therapy as effective as in-person sessions?

Research shows that properly conducted online physical therapy can be just as effective as in-person therapy for many conditions. Our therapists are specially trained in remote guidance techniques to ensure you receive optimal care.

What if I don't have all the recommended equipment?

Our therapists are skilled at adapting exercises using common household items. During your initial assessment, we'll inventory what you have available and customize your program accordingly. We can also suggest affordable equipment options if needed.

How do you ensure proper form and technique remotely?

We use multi-angle viewing when possible and provide detailed verbal cues. Our therapists are experts at visual assessment and can quickly spot and correct form issues. We also use video recordings when helpful for more complex movements.

Is insurance accepted for online physical therapy?

Many insurance providers now cover telehealth physical therapy sessions. We can verify your benefits prior to beginning treatment. We also offer affordable self-pay options and packages for those without coverage.
